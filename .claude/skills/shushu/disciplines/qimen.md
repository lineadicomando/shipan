# 奇門遁甲 qímén dùnjiǎ — board `qimen`

Researched 2026-10-23. The register (`docs/sources.md`) is where this file's
claims about the shelf come from. Wherever this file quotes a web page it says
`(web transcription)`, and every such quote is a lead and never a witness.
Quotations taken from the shelf's own transcriptions (`texts/qimen/*.txt`)
are marked `(shelf transcription)`. They are finding aids too, until someone
reads them on the plate. Simplified transcriptions are quoted here in
traditional characters.

## 1. What the art is

- **Scope.** 遁甲 dùnjiǎ ("the concealed 甲") lays one board of nine palaces
  for an instant. That board has an earth plate (地盤 dìpán) of nine stems,
  a heaven plate (天盤 tiānpán) turned to the hour, nine stars (九星 jiǔxīng),
  eight gates (八門 bāmén) and eight spirits (八神 bāshén). 甲 is hidden under
  the six 儀 yí (戊己庚辛壬癸), and the three 奇 qí (乙丙丁) are the
  "marvels". The board answers **a question asked at an instant** (卜 bǔ in
  this project's terms). It is not about a birth, and a birth enters it only
  as a 年命 niánmìng placed inside it. Its classical object was war: direction,
  host and guest (主客 zhǔkè), the hour to move. 「天乙之神所在宮，大將宜居擊對衝」
  is a line of the 煙波釣叟歌 (shelf transcription).
- **Families.** The hour board, 時家 shíjiā, is the one practised. The day
  (日家 rìjiā), month (月家 yuèjiā) and year (年家 niánjiā) boards are
  stated by 《遁甲演義》 and the 統宗 (see `system`). A modern minute board,
  刻家 kèjiā or 分家 fēnjiā, is offered by software.
- **History, as the texts let it be dated.**
  - 後漢書·方術傳 lists 遁甲 among the Han arts in its preface, and says of
    高獲 「曉遁甲，能役使鬼神」 (web transcription). It is a name and nothing
    more: no procedure survives.
  - 葛洪 Gě Hóng (283–343), 抱朴子·登涉: 「余少有入山之志，由此乃行學遁甲書，乃有六十餘卷」,
    and 「凡六癸為天藏，六己為地戶也」 (web transcription). That is an early,
    magical-hemerological 遁甲 for entering mountains.
  - 隋書·經籍志 lists some fifty-five 遁甲 titles. Among them are 伍子胥's
    遁甲文, 葛洪's 遁甲返覆圖 and 遁甲要用, 臨孝恭's 遁甲立成法, 信都芳's
    遁甲 in 33 juan, 三元遁甲 by 許昉 and by 劉毗, 遁甲九宮八門圖, and 遁甲九宮亭亭白奸書
    (web transcription). "三元", "九宮" and "八門" are therefore attested as
    title words by the 7th century.
  - 李筌 Lǐ Quán, 神機制敵太白陰經 (c. 759), 卷九 遁甲, is **the earliest
    received procedure**. It gives the nine palaces, the eight gates and nine
    stars by number, 「常以直符加時干」, three 元 from the first 甲子 after
    each solstice, and term rows such as 「艮 立春八五二」 and 「震 春分三九六」
    (web transcription). Its preface writes 「凡一千八百局，名曰《天乙遁甲式》」.
  - Northern Song. 宋史·藝文志 lists 僧一行's 遁甲十八局 and 楊惟德 Yáng
    Wéidé's 景祐遁甲符應經 in 3 juan (1034–38), beside some twenty-five more
    遁甲 titles (web transcription). 武經總要 後集 卷二十一 (1044; 曾公亮,
    丁度, 楊惟德) states a complete 遁甲法. Its 二十四氣布局立成 is the
    ordinary table, and the passage is quoted in §3 and §8. 《崇寧國子監算學令》
    (1102–06) set the 三式 for the 司天監's students (web transcription,
    via zh.wikipedia).
  - Ming. 《奇門遁甲統宗》 is the compilation the 池本理 line is catalogued
    under. 程道生's 《遁甲演義》 (c. 1613) entered the 四庫. The 煙波釣叟歌
    is "a Northern Song verse, revised by 明 羅通" by Wikisource's own note.
    The name 奇門遁甲 becomes usual from the Ming on.
  - Qing. Two imperial prints, 《御定奇門寶鑑》 and 《奇門遁甲金鏡寶鑑》. The
    《御定奇門大全》 compilation, sixty-four juan, has a 跋 dated 1780. 甘國壁's
    《折衷》 (after 1680). 紀大奎's 《仕學備餘》 (after 1796).
  - The flying lineage surfaces in print in the 同治 years (§2).

## 2. Texts and lineages

★ = on the shelf (`texts/README.md`). ☆ = wanted and not held (`TODO.md`).

**Classical and imperial**

- ★ 《奇門遁甲統宗》, the 池本理 line, held four ways: 故宮珍本叢刊 第426冊,

  It holds most of the defaults (`plate: zhuan`, `centreLodging: kun`,
  `method: zhirun`, `leap: solstice`, `spiritSeat: tianpan`, `dingTomb: gen`,
  `menpo: menzhigong`, `deshi: liuyi`). 卷十二 carries the natal reading the
  engine refuses.
- ★ 《御定奇門寶鑑》, 六卷, 故宮 第431冊, with a typeset edition and 遁甲集成

  `spirits: fixed`.

  `leap: runyue` and `strengths: star`. Its 卷一 (series p. 269) buries 丁 in

- ★ 《遁甲符應經》, the 景祐 commission (楊惟德), in three copies. Holds
  `dingTomb: qian`.
- ★ 《御定奇門大全》, 64 juan, surveyed whole. 景祐秘纂 卷二 is the 符應經's
  卷中 again, with three cases that lay only under `centreStem: host`. It

  奇門仙機 (its 干論, `stemPairs: xianji`, seventy-seven cells read on the

- ★ 《奇門遁甲秘笈大全》, 題 劉伯溫, held in 故宮 第427冊, the 省思堂 blocks, a
  transcription and 張志春's 詳解. Its 卷十五 verse holds `yiTomb: qian`.
- ★ 《金函玉鏡奇門遁甲秘笈全書》 (1914 lithograph, reset 1996). `stemPairs:
  jinhan`, and the 日家 of `system: rijia`.
- ★ 《奇門遁甲折衷》, 甘國壁 (清). Holds `plate: xingfei`, `earthPlate: xun`
  and `centreLodging: bajie`/`siyu`. It names 顧陵岡池本理 for the turn and
  the 坤 lodging, and rejects them.
- ★ 《紀氏奇門秘書仕學備餘》, 紀大奎 (after 1796). The 歌 with 舊註, 甘氏 and
  池氏, and a sentence rejecting 拆補 (「後人據之拆補者謬也」).
- ★ 《奇門探索錄》 (Republican; 2012 華齡 print, and an eight-juan text). Its

  the source of the engine's hexagrams.
- ★ 《奇門遁甲元靈經》, 許松如, preface 1883. Transcription only, of
  unstated provenance. Its worked examples carry the `centreStem` question,

- ★ 遁甲集成 第一–六冊 (廣陵). 第三冊 holds the 年家 on 一七四.
- ★ 《黃帝太一八門逆順生死訣》 and 《入式秘訣》, 正統道藏. The gate-errand
  verse and its 呪.
- ★ the 煙波釣叟歌, in the Wikisource 羅通 recension and inside five
  compilations (§8).
- ★ 《御定奇門真詮》: 1080 boards, no prose.
- 神機制敵太白陰經 卷九 (唐 李筌): the earliest procedure. Wikisource

- 武經總要 後集 卷二十一 (1044): Song state compilation. Wikisource
  transcription only.
- 虎鈐經 (許洞, 1004) 卷十二 has a 遁甲遊都. That is a 六壬-style 遊都 off the
  月將 and not a board, and it is irrelevant to 奇門's 起例 (web
  transcription).
- ☆ judged worth having, not bought (TODO §2): 《宮藏奇門大全》,
  《遁甲奇門秘傳要旨》, 《明抄奇門陰遁書》, and 《李衛公奇門心法》 (with a Tang

**The flying lineage (飛盤 fēipán)**, none of it on the shelf, all of it
web leads:

- 《奇門法竅》, 錫孟樨 Xī Mèngxī (or 錫孟穉), 同治九年 (1870), circulated in

## 3. How a board is laid (起例)

Each step names the engine parameter, or says "not modelled".

1. **The instant into pillars.** True solar time (`trueSolarTime: true`),
   day turned at 子時 (`dayBoundary: zishi` against `midnight`), year at
   立春 (`yearBoundary`). Practitioners split on all three. Mainland software
   commonly uses 真太陽時; fengshui-hacks reads clock time on 120°E and turns
   the day at 23:00 (register). The term is the instant the Sun reaches
   15°·n.
2. **Dun 遁 and the 局 jú (定局 dìngjú).** Yang from 冬至, yin from 夏至.
   Each term opens on a palace (「春從艮起夏當巽，秋過坤宮冬到乾」, 禽遁 in
   the 大全), and the 局 table runs 冬至 一七四 … 大雪 四七一. That table is
   stated alike from 太白陰經 (c. 759) through 武經總要 (1044) and the 大全,
   with one slip at 芒種 in 奇門秘訣. How the 元 yuán is chosen is `method`:
   - 拆補 chāibǔ (`chaibu`, default). The term in force at the instant, and
     the 元 from the day's 符頭 fútóu: 甲 or 己 on 子午卯酉 gives 上元, on
     寅申巳亥 中元, on 辰戌丑未 下元 (「甲己在仲為上局，孟為中局季下元」). Held by
     張志春. 紀大奎 rejects it by name. 《奇門法竅》 is said to accept it
     (web lead, §7).
   - 置閏 zhìrùn (`zhirun`). Whole 符頭 blocks of fifteen days. When the 符頭
     runs ahead of the term (超神 chāoshén) by nine days or more, a block is
     repeated before a solstice (`leap: solstice`, the block at 芒種 or 大雪;
     `leap: runyue`, under the term of the leap month, refused). Held by the
     統宗 and the 大全. The pin at nine (`MAX_CHAOSHEN` = 8) is the 統宗's, the
     大全 卷十三's and 奇門秘訣's. The 大全 卷十二 says ten
     (「凡過超十日則閏」): not modelled.
   - 茅山 máoshān (`maoshan`). 上元 from the instant the term begins, five days
     each, with no 符頭. Held by 劉文元 and 唐頤.
   - 陰盤 (王鳳麟): numbers mod 9. Not modelled (§7).
   - 太白陰經: 「陽遁，冬至後，第一甲子爲上元…」. The 元 opens on the first
     甲子 after the solstice. Whether that is a 接氣-only rule, unlike 置閏's
     超神, is not modelled and not established (§7).
3. **The earth plate.** Three 奇 and six 儀 through the 洛書 order, with the
   儀 forward and the 奇 backward under yang, reversed under yin. It is laid
   once for the 元 (`earthPlate: yuan`) or anew at each decade, 六戊還原
   (`xun`, the 折衷's, which it puts on 豫章張氏).
4. **The 旬首 xúnshǒu and 符首.** The hour's decade names the 儀 hiding 甲
   (甲子戊, 甲戌己, 甲申庚, 甲午辛, 甲辰壬, 甲寅癸). The star at that 儀's
   palace is the 值符 zhífú. The gate at that palace is the 值使 zhíshǐ.
5. **The centre (寄宮 jìgōng).** Where the fifth is read is `centreLodging`:
   - `kun`, both dun at 坤 (統宗, 寶鑑卷二 「陽遁陰遁俱寄坤宮」, 符應經).
     武經總要: 「天禽中五寄坤二宮」 (web transcription).
   - `dun`, yang 坤 and yin 艮 (漢陰居士歌; the 寶鑑 reports it).
   - `dunni`, the reverse (the 折衷 quoting 三才發秘 and a 元經).
   - `bajie`, through all eight palaces by term.
   - `siyu`, through the four corners.

   When the count lands in the centre, the 甲辰 decade takes 天禽 and 死門
   (「甲辰在中宮，寄於坤二，天禽為符，死門為使」).
   - Whether the pair is reported at the host or in the fifth:
     `centreTravel` (`stay` against `travel`, 金鏡寶鑑 「符使不必寄於二」).
   - Whether the centre's stem rides the turn with the host's stem:
     `centreStem` (`centre` against `host`, the 大全's tables, the 演義's
     刑格 and 飛干格, and 元靈經).

   The 探索錄's four readings of what is *read* in the centre (a gate of its
   own, every spirit there, read with the 值使門) are not modelled.
6. **The heaven plate.** Under 轉 zhuàn (`plate: zhuan`), the 值符 star with
   its 儀 goes to the palace of the hour's stem (「值符常遣加時干」), and the
   ring of eight turns. Under 飛 (`fei`, refused), every layer flies through
   nine in 洛書 order. The 折衷's hybrid, stars and 儀 flown and gates turned,
   is `xingfei`. If the hour's stem is 甲, it is read as its 儀.
7. **The gates.** The 值使 counts from its palace by the hours of the decade
   (「值使順逆遁宮去」; 武經總要 「以直事加時宮」), forward under yang and
   backward under yin through the nine numbers, lodging when it reaches 五.
   The other seven follow round the eight. In the flying line, the 鳴法's
   gates fly through nine (web lead).
8. **The spirits.** 值符 螣蛇 太陰 六合 then the middle pair, then 九地 九天.
   They run forward under yang and backward under yin (煙波:
   「值符前三六合位，太陰之神在前二。後一宮中為九天，後二之神為九地」).
   - The seat is `spiritSeat`: `tianpan`, or `yinshi`, from the earth-plate
     值符 at 己–癸 hours.
   - The names of the fifth and sixth are `spirits`:
     - `dun` (勾陳/朱雀 under yang, 白虎/玄武 under yin);
     - `fixed`;
     - `baihu`;
     - `huwu` (白虎 and 玄武 in both dun, the 大全's tables and fengshui-hacks).
   - Their phase, for a lens, is `spiritPhase`: `none`, `branch` (default since
     2026-10-25), `huaqi`. It moves no seat and no name.
   - The flying line uses nine spirits, adding 太常 (web lead). Not modelled.
   - A modern convention that names the spirits by the matter is refused
     (`docs/agent-prompt.md`).
9. **States 旺相休囚死.** By the season (`strengths: season`), or from the
   star (`star`, 金鏡寶鑑's arrangement, refused). A second arrangement read
   from the star belongs to the 《三元經》 that the 符應經, 紀大奎 and the
   探索錄 carry, and to the 歌's own couplet 「與我同行即為相，我生之月誠為旺。
   廢於父母休於財，囚於鬼兮真不妄」. It is not modelled. Earth's season is
   `earth: quarters` or `eighteen`.
10. **Configurations (格 gé).** They are read off the finished plates (§4).
    Where the texts split, the split is a parameter: `dingTomb`, `yiTomb`,
    `menpo`, `deshi`, `stemPairs`.
11. **年命.** Optional. 本命 and 行年 are placed inside the board, as
    《遁甲演義》 has them: 行年 opens at 丙寅 forward for a man and 壬申
    backward for a woman.
12. **Other families** (`system`):
    - `rijia` is computed: eight gates three days to a palace skipping the
      centre, and the nine 太乙 stars one palace a day. `dayGates` takes
      `fei`, `zhuan` or `shun`. `dayDun` takes `solstice` or `yang`;
      `liushi` is refused.
    - `yuejia` and `nianjia` are refused. The year family's three 元 are
      一四七 for the 演義 and the 統宗, and 一七四 for 遁甲集成 第三冊 and the
      大全's 奇門古本.

## 4. How a board is read

The engine refuses everything in this section. A consultant gives it, and
says whose reading it is.

**What is looked at first.**

- **The 煙波釣叟歌**, the common root:
  - the 值符 and the 值使 come first (「更得值符值使利，兵家用事最為貴」);
  - then the three 奇 with the three good gates (開 休 生) and 太陰
    (「奇與門兮共太陰，三般難得共加臨；若還得二亦為吉」);
  - 「急從神兮緩從門」: in haste go by the spirit, at leisure by the gate.
- **The 統宗** (shelf transcription) reads by the four stems as kin:
  「年干為父，月干為兄弟，日干為己身，時干為子孫，或以生我之干為父母，我生之干為子孫，比和之干為兄弟，天上時干為己身」.
  The 探索錄 quotes a 《大全》 for 「年干為父母，月干為兄弟，時干為妻子，又庚為夫，乙為妻」.
- **The mainland school** (幺學聲 2005, shelf transcription):
  - 年干 stands for parents and superiors, 月干 for siblings, colleagues and
    partners, 日干 for oneself, 時干 for children and subordinates;
  - 「日干為預測之人，時干為所測之事」;
  - the 年命 is checked beside them.
  - The same doctrine stands in the abridgement under 張志春's name: 「測天時，以九星為主；測人事，以八門為主；測地理、方位，以九宮為主；測六親，以年、月、日、時為主」.
    It says any of sixty-four symbols can be a 用神. It treats the 用神 method
    as the main one of four (the others are reading the whole board, 六親, and
    年命).

**Choosing the 用神 yòngshén by matter**, attributed. These are the
consultant's to choose, never the engine's.

The gate errands that travel in the engine's `PURPOSES` are the ones two
independent witnesses share (`docs/sources.md` § 八門). The modern
widenings, 驚門 for litigation and 生門 for treatment, belong to the manuals
and not to the classical texts.

**Strength.** The states of the 用神's star and gate (`starStrength`,
`gateStrength`). The palace's relation to its gate and star (`gateRelation`,
`starRelation`, with 門迫 read from the gate's). The 統宗 卷二 has
「吉門有氣益吉，無氣減吉；凶門有氣益凶，無氣減凶」. The 歌 on stars:

- 「輔禽心星為上吉，衝任小吉未全亨。大凶蓬芮不堪使，小凶英柱不精明。小凶無氣變為吉，大凶無氣卻平平」.

Mainland practice (幺學聲) adds three more reads:

- 橫看, the five-phase relation between the palaces of the 用神 and the
  querent: 「日干克時干，指的是日干所落的宮位克時干所落的宮位」;
- whether the 用神 stands in the inner plate (內盤, the four palaces of the
  dun's own half) or the outer;
- the twelve stages of the stem in its palace.

**Timing (應期).** 幺學聲's checklist, in order: if the 用神's palace is void,
the date comes when the void is struck or filled (沖空, 填實). If it holds the
post horse, the date comes when the horse moves or is struck. If it is in its
tomb, the date comes when the tomb is opened or entered. 刑 is released by a
合 and 合 by a 沖. Otherwise go by the twelve stages. A 值使 in 震 points to
three units, and so on. 伏吟 is slow and 反吟 fast. The 張志春 abridgement adds
a 庚格 timing: 「陰日看庚上之干，陽日看庚下之干」. 元靈經's 定用神訣 orders
events by the palace numbers (「數少者為先」). The engine refuses all dating
(`docs/refusals.md`).

**Host and guest.** 「甲乙丙丁戊陽時…坐擊須憑天上奇，陰時地下亦如之」. The
three 奇 in yang hours favour the guest, who moves first; in yin hours, the
host, who answers (統宗: 「五陽時…將兵利客，宜先舉」). Strike from the 值符's
palace at its opposite (「常從此地擊其衝」). 三勝 and 五不擊 are listed in
武經總要 and 禽遁 (「天乙真符直使九天九地出門皆不可擊」).

**The common 格**, with the line that names each. The engine computes those
marked ●.

- ● 伏吟 / 返吟: 「天蓬加著地天蓬」 / 「天蓬若到天英上」. The 歌 calls both 凶.
  Modern readings: 伏吟 is still and delayed, 返吟 is reversal and speed.
- ● 六儀擊刑: 「甲子值符愁向東。戌刑未上申刑虎，寅巳辰辰午刑午」. These are
  the six of `STRIKE`, and 吉凶格解 and 武經總要 agree. What is struck is the
  hour's 直符 where it is carried on the heaven plate (符應經 p. 430,
  「以甲子直符加六庚於三宮」), never a 儀 lying on the earth plate; and, by the
  same page, the hour whose branch its decade's head punishes (「日出時」, 丁卯),
  which the engine marks without a palace.
- ● 三奇入墓 (`dingTomb`, `yiTomb`, §8). 時干入墓: 「戊戌壬辰與壬癸，癸未丁丑亦同凶」
  is not modelled.
- ● 五不遇時: 「時干來剋日干上」, the hour stem controlling the day stem at
  the same polarity, derived.
- ● 門迫: 「宮制其門不為迫，門制其宮是迫雄」 is the 歌's (Wikisource) clause
  for `menzhigong`. 吉凶格解 names the converse (`gongzhimen`).
- ● 三奇得使: 「乙逢犬馬丙鼠猴，六丁玉女騎龍虎」 is `liuyi`. 武經總要 states the
  same: 「甲戌、甲午乙為使，甲子、甲申丙為使，甲寅、甲辰丁為使」 (web
  transcription). `zhishi` is 張志春's.
- ● 青龍返首 戊+丙 and 飛鳥跌穴 丙+戊 (吉). 太白入熒 庚+丙 and 熒入太白 丙+庚
  (「白入熒兮賊即來，熒入白兮賊即去」). 大格 庚+癸, 刑格 庚+己, 上格/小格 庚+壬.
  螣蛇夭矯 癸+丁, 朱雀投江 丁+癸, 青龍逃走 乙+辛, 白虎猖狂 辛+乙. Plus the
  `stemPairs` tables: the default's sixteen, 青龍入雲格 戊+乙 and 青龍華蓋 戊+癸
  among them with no fortune; 《金函玉鏡》's eighty; the 干論's seventy-seven.
- 玉女守門 (「三奇遊六儀」, the 值使 over 丁). 天遁, 地遁 and 人遁, and the
  other six 遁 of the manuals. 天三門 and 地四戶. 天網四張: 「六癸」 for the 統宗,
  癸 over the hour stem for 吉凶格解. 伏干, 飛干, 天乙伏宮 and 飛宮. 悖格: 丙
  over the year, month, day or hour stem or the 值符. 歲月日時格: 庚 over those
  stems. 三詐 and 五假. 天輔時. None of these is modelled.
- ● 空亡 carries 吉凶 both ways (「凶格落空則凶不成」). ● The post horses
  (日馬, 時馬).

**The phases of the spirits are the old texts' too, and they part.** The
奇門秘訣 (《御定奇門大全》卷四十七, sheet 1086) gives each spirit the phase of

`none`, `branch`, `huaqi`, all read on the plate 2026-10-25. Long 類象 lists
of things, organs and persons follow in the modern manuals, and those are a
modern school's.

## 5. Pitfalls

- **Method switching.** 拆補 and 茅山 disagree about three hours in five,
  and 置閏 can lay a term the Sun has not reached. Never compare boards
  across methods.
- **茅山 sold as 拆補.** The engine did this for a whole phase
  (`docs/history/40-the-default-was-maoshan.md`). A 拆補 that does not read
  the 符頭 is 茅山.
- **Software 置閏 tables** pin the leap at eight, nine or ten days. Within
  the 大全, 卷十二 and 卷十三 part by one day.
- **Day boundary.** Many tables and most English software turn the day at
  23:00 on clock time without true solar time. A 子時 board can flip both the
  day stem, and with it the 符頭 and so the 元, and the hour stem.
- **The 120°E lunar calendar** governs nothing in 奇門's 局, which runs on
  solar terms. 陰盤 does use the lunar month and day, so a 陰盤 board depends
  on whose lunar calendar it uses.
- **Spirit names.** 白虎/玄武 against 勾陳/朱雀 and 太常 differ between
  books and apps. That is the `spirits` axis, not an error.
- **"Nine gates" in 飛盤.** Wikipedia and apps say 飛宮 adds a 中門. The
  鳴法 page says 「中五無門」. Do not assume.
- **天禽 and 芮禽.** A board drawn without a centre cell prints 天芮禽
  together under every lodging convention. That shows nothing about the
  convention (register).
- **三奇得使's two rules**, and 幺學聲's wording 「天盤乙丙丁加臨地盤值使門」,
  which may be a third (§8).
- **門迫's direction.** The name is reversed between 統宗 and 吉凶格解. Read
  the board's `menpo`.
- **入墓 by the twelve stages.** 乙's stage-tomb is 戌 (乾). The 奇門 tables
  put it in 坤. Do not "fix" `TOMB`.
- **English terminology.** "Structure" (Joey Yap) is a 局 or a 格. "Deity"
  is a 八神. "Door" is a 門. "Hour Qi Men" is 時家. "Destiny Qi Men" is a
  natal reading this project refuses. "Imperial Qi Men" is used loosely in
  English for 置閏.
- **Recalled tables are wrong more often than not** (CLAUDE.md). The 入墓
  couplet alone circulates in five readings (§8).

  The refusal row's reason may want re-reading when one arrives.
- **幺學聲's 三奇得使 may not be `zhishi`.** He writes 「天盤乙、丙、丁加臨地盤值使門」
  (shelf transcription): a 奇 over the earth-plate palace of the 值使 gate.
  開悟之門 instead puts the 奇 in the palace where the 值使 gate stands now
  (「門盤是值使門」). These are two cells whenever the 值使 has moved. The
  register says 幺學聲 «gives the 值使 rule» and was not quoted. Read his
  page before naming him beside `zhishi`.
