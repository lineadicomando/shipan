# Many lines and no orthodoxy: the premise, and the transit art by art

**This page is a proposal and binds nothing.** It is the one page in this
directory that does not describe the project as it is: it argues a change of
premise under `sources.md`, says what that change would move in every art the
engine lays, and sets out the stages. When the change lands, what holds goes to
the pages that own it, the record goes to `history/`, and this file goes.

Written 2026-10-07, against version 0.6.0. It replaces the draft of the same
day, which argued only for admitting a school's modern manual and only for the
values already declared. Every count here is a reading taken that day and none
is asserted by a test.

**What this page was read off, and what it was not.** No plate was opened for
it. Every statement about a text is one the register already makes, with one
exception said where it occurs: 《奇門遁甲折衷》 was surveyed whole on the
transcription sold with it, which `sources.md` files as a finding aid, so what
is new about that work here *locates* and establishes nothing. The five arts
other than 奇門 were surveyed from `sources.md`, `sources.tsv`,
`parameters.ts`, `parameters.md` and `ROADMAP.md` § 1, and a sample of the
lines leaned on was read back.

## In short

- **The premise that goes.** That comparing witnesses recovers the rule of an
  art, so that agreement between two is the threshold and belonging to one
  line is a risk. The shelf answered otherwise in every art: the arts are
  branched and layered from as far back as the texts reach.
- **The premise that replaces it.** A transmitted quantity asserts **who
  carries it and how well it was transcribed**. Divergence between lines is
  the subject of the register and not a defect in it. «The answer» remains
  only for what is measured or derived.
- **The architecture already agrees.** Parameters, declared defaults, the
  school in force stated on every board, «registered as one school among
  others». What does not agree is how the register weighs: one sentence of the
  standard, the gloss of rung 2, the meaning of `—`, and the reasons several
  values are refused.
- **It is not a 奇門 matter.** Each art carries the old premise in a form of
  its own, set out below, and the transit is run in each.
- **The plan is gradual and local.** A dedicated branch, never pushed, one
  commit a step; shared mechanisms first and once, then the same pass
  repeated art by art.

## The premise, stated

**What changes is one sentence and what hangs from it.** `sources.md` § "The
standard, stated once" says «For anything that cannot be derived, **two** such
sources must agree». Read as the threshold for *shipping* it was never applied
— eleven rows stand at rung 5 — and read as the threshold for *truth* it is the
old premise whole.

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| What a transmitted quantity asserts | «this is the rule» | «this line carries it, and here it is transcribed» |
| What two agreeing works are | the standard | a check: each tests the other's transcription. Who shares a reading is a separate fact, carried per value |
| What one line alone is | a risk the ladder orders | the ordinary case |
| What two disagreeing works are | «neither of the two things the standard accepts» | two values, each attributed |
| What a default is | the received reading | a convention: an address naming nothing still answers, and it stays put so that saved answers do |
| What `—` says | «the tradition's own» | no divergence known on the shelf that was asked |
| What «the answer» covers | everything not named for a school | what is measured or derived: rungs 0, 1 and 3 |

**What does not change.**

- **An edition can only be preferred.** Plurality of lines is not plurality of
  misprints; a disagreement between witnesses to one text is still settled
  once in the register.
- **Every refusal in `refusals.md`.** They already rest on «the schools
  divide».
- **A valence travels where the lines consulted agree**, and now for a stated
  reason: there the concordance is itself the datum.
- **Reading on the plate, and the extract as a finding aid.**
- **One board and one school in a prompt.**

### The ladder, kept and corrected

1. **Rung 2 is redefined**: two independent texts carry the same reading, and
   each checks the other's transcription. «Transmitted» goes — `sihua: zuofu`
   already stands there on two modern manuals — and so does «this is the
   standard».
2. **The sentence of the standard leaves** `sources.md` and the gloss
   `notes.rung.2.means`.
3. **A line says why a collation stays off the ladder** now that the ladder
   orders transcription: a second copy inherits its archetype's errors and an
   independent work does not.

`notes.md` already says the ladder orders «what could go wrong unnoticed»; the
correction is to the one place it adds «that a rule handed down is the one
lineage's own».

## The mechanisms, built once for every art

| Mechanism | What it is | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Three states of a divergence | computed · declared and refused · recorded with no parameter. `ROADMAP.md` § 1 has all three today; the transit says which state each recorded divergence is in, and why | `ROADMAP.md` § 1, one sub-table an art |
| A registry of witnesses | identifier, hanzi, pinyin, author, year, and whether it is a transmitted work, a modern manual or the engine's own construction. Works are named today in prose across several modules | `packages/core` |
| Who holds each value | on every `ParameterValue`, the witnesses that hold it, held by a test to `sources.tsv` | `parameters.ts` |
| The combination line | derived from the two above, in the three states of decision 7; printed where the school in force is printed | `format.ts`, the prompt's fence, the drawing's caption, the page, REST, MCP |
| A `school` cell that can name several values | a row such as the heaven plate stands under `plate`, `centreLodging` and `spirits` at once, and the test admits one pair a cell | `sources.tsv`, `docs.test.ts` |

The registry answers a safeguard of the earlier draft by derivation: a value
standing on a manual of 1986 and one standing on a Ming print are told apart
by the year of the witness, not by prose in `stands_on`.

**The risk these are built against is the board nobody cast.** Axes are
independent in the engine and lines hold them together; every axis added
multiplies the combinations no text lays. The combination line says so of each
board, as a statement about the shelf and never as a refusal.

## The transit, art by art

Five forms of the old premise recur. Each art shows one or two of them.

| Form | Where |
|---|---|
| Agreement is the rule | every art: the standard |
| One work is the measure | 紫微斗數 (《全書》), 曆注 (《協紀辨方書》), 太乙 (《金鏡式經》), 奇門 (the 統宗 line) |
| What two programs agree on is «the transmitted board» | 六壬, and in part 紫微斗數 and 奇門 |
| Astronomy is the measure | 七政四餘 |
| Two works filed as two copies, or a plural transmission filed as a reason to refuse | 太乙; 奇門's three other families |

### 奇門

**Where the premise shows.** The defaults `plate: zhuan` and `centreLodging:
kun` are one party's doctrine — the 折衷 names 顧陵岡池本理 for the pair, and the
register says every witness for either sits in that line — and no row of
`sources.tsv` stands under either value. The year, month and day families are
refused because their fullest witness finds the transmission plural:
「諸說紛紜，全無定見」.

|---|---|---|

| a new earth plate at every decade (六戊還原) | located, weighed at nothing | **declared**: a parameter of its own |
| the centre lodged through eight palaces, or four corners | the 折衷's own quarrel | **declared**: two further values of `centreLodging` |
| the 值使 taken from the earth plate or from the heaven plate | not in the register | **recorded**: the 折衷 chooses between them by the matter asked, and the engine answers no question |

| `system: nianjia` on 一四七 against 一七四 | refused on a reading | **two values**, by decision 10, when the family is built |
| `strengths: star` | one text tabulating its own rule | **reachable**: a work arguing its own reading is its own line; whether the rule reaches gates and spirits is still to be read |
| `leap: runyue` | attribution wanted | stays: the third text suggests the two values are one rule |
| `system: yuejia`, `earth: eighteen` | refused | stay: one line contradicting itself; no text |

Two leads the transcription of the 折衷 gives and the register does not carry,

## The stages

**Executed locally, on a branch of its own.** `plural-lines`, cut from `main`
at stage 0 and never pushed. One commit a step, as `CLAUDE.md` asks off
`main`, each with a subject saying what the code does. What becomes of the
series when the branch lands is decided at stage 9. A version bump rides in
the commit that earned it, by `architecture.md`'s three questions.

Shared mechanisms come first and once. Then the same pass runs in every art,
in an order set by how much the shelf already holds.

| | Stage | Arts | Touches | Version |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | **The premise.** The standard's sentence, the ladder's three corrections, `—`, the default as a convention; `parameters.md`, the lines in `CLAUDE.md`, the opening of `ROADMAP.md` § 1, the glosses on the notes pages in every vernacular; a phase file saying which premise was dropped | all | docs, catalogs | third term |
| 1 | **The register's grammar.** A `school` cell naming several values; the registry of witnesses; who holds each value. Nothing a caller sees | all | `core`, tests | none |
| 2 | **The attribution pass**, one commit an art: the holder of every default, every `—` re-read, the rows owed, each recorded divergence put in one of the three states in `ROADMAP.md` § 1 | 紫微斗數 → 奇門 → 六壬 → 太乙 → 七政四餘 → 曆注 → pillars, 八字, 年命 | register, registry | third term |
| 3 | **The combination line** on every surface | all | `core`, i18n, plate, web, MCP, CLI | second term |
| 4 | **What the shelf already allows**, one commit a value | 紫微斗數 `daxian: ming`, then `huoling: hour`; 奇門 `centreLodging: dun` | the `new-feature` procedure | second term each |
| 5 | **The ladder weighed again**, with three single-witness school values in the register | all | a decision, perhaps `notes.md` | — |
| 6 | **The shelf, with closed questions.** No code. The 折衷's three chapters and its two leads; which of 關 and 掩 the 統宗 of 太乙 holds; whose `huoling: hour` is if stage 4 left it open; whether anybody holds 涉害 read in the verse's own order; one targeted search for each value still waiting | 奇門, 太乙, 六壬 | `sources.md`, `ROADMAP.md` | third term |
| 7 | **The declarations owed**, one movement an art, each value named for its rule and refused until computed | 奇門, then 太乙, then 紫微斗數 | input types, every surface | second term each |
| 8 | **Values of new axes, and the data-bound ones**, one commit a value | as stage 6 allows; 七政四餘 `xiudu: shoushi` | the `new-feature` procedure | second term each |
| 9 | **Landing**, and what waits beyond it: a family of `system`, 太乙's other registers as the 統宗's | — | — | — |

**What each stage is checked by.**

- Stage 0: `npm test`; the sweep of state phrases `ROADMAP.md` § "Resuming
  cold" lists, widened to «received», «the standard asks» and «the tradition's
  own».
- Stage 1: a test that fails where a value has no holder and no «not yet
  attributed»; a test holding holders to the register.
- Stage 2: an art is done when it has no `—` on a quantity a parameter moves,
  and no default without either a holder or the word that it has none.
- Stage 3: the canonical fascicle does not move; the transcripts do, and the
  tests pinning them are updated in the same commit.
- Stage 4: the worked examples the witness prints, as tests; for `huoling:
  hour`, agreement with `iztro` over the 544 births rising from a quarter to
  the whole.
- Stage 7: every new value refused by name on every surface, and each board
  unchanged under its defaults.

**Why the attribution pass runs across all the arts before any value is
computed.** It is the stage that shows whether the premise holds outside 奇門,
it costs no code, and it is where the combination line gets the data without
which stage 3 would print «not yet attributed» on every board.

**Where it can stop.** After stage 0 the project says one thing about itself.
After stage 2 every art has been through the transit and no answer has
changed. After stage 4 everything the shelf holds today is spent. Stages 6 to
8 are new reading and new code, and can wait without leaving anything half
done.

## Risks

| Risk | Safeguard |
|---|---|
| A board no line ever cast, served as though one had | the combination line, in every answer |
| The default combination itself turns out to be nobody's | accepted: it is what «a default is a convention» means, and the line says it |
| Plurality becomes a licence to compute anything written down | decision 2: attribution, cell by cell, without contradiction, on the plate |
| A hostile witness misreports what it rejects | the value says who reports it and travels with that witness's name; a holder found later replaces it |
| A single-witness value cannot be run against anything | the witness's own worked examples are the check; where they fail on their own terms, as the 折衷's dated example does, the entry says so |
| A modern manual presents a synthesis as ancient | the value asserts «this line teaches this» and never an age; the year of the witness is in the registry |
| The reader meets more choices than they can weigh | controls stay under the disclosure; what stands in the open is the statement of what was followed |
| The register is rewritten to fit the premise | nothing is: an entry is reopened when its value is taken up, and says what it used to stand on |
