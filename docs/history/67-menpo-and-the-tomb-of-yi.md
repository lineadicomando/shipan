## What was built

**`menpo`**, which relation of a gate to its palace carries the name 門迫.
`menzhigong`, the default, is what the engine always computed. `gongzhimen`
is 《吉凶格解》's: the palace controlling its gate is 門迫, and the gate
controlling its palace is marked 宮迫, a new configuration `gongpo` with the
fortune the work gives it. The relation each palace reports does not move,
and a test holds the marks to it under both values.

**`yiTomb`**, the palace 乙 is buried in, an axis apart from `dingTomb`
because each text names the palace of each 奇. `kun` is the default, and
`qian` is the verse of 《奇門遁甲秘笈大全》卷十五 and 奇門秘訣's 三璋. The
秘笈大全 enters the witnesses as `mijidaquan`. With 乙 an axis of its own, it
and 奇門秘訣 also hold `dingTomb: gen`.

Both cross the surfaces as `spiritSeat` did: the two catalogs, the form, the
CLI and `yi_tomb` and `menpo` for agents. The canonical 奇門 board gains the
two options and no mark. 0.30.0.

## What the re-reading found

The refusal said the sources disagree on which pairings count. Five works on
the shelf give one pairing: the 統宗's 四十格, the 符應經, the 遁甲演義, 吉凶格解
and the verse. The 演義's copy of the 葛洪 cases lays on the engine, where the
符應經's copy did not. What is disputed is two other things. The first is the
rule itself: 張志春's 開悟之門 makes 得使 a 奇 with the 值使 gate, and calls
that his provisional opinion. The second is the fortune of three of the six
cells, which the 演義 calls 不吉 unless the decade's 直符 stands there. The
reason as written does not hold, and nothing was built on the re-reading. It
waits on the user's word.
