# Phase 70 — 景祐秘纂 卷二–四

The last of the 大全, begun 2026-10-23 and interrupted before the write-up,
finished 2026-10-24 at the user's «riprendi la lettura dello scaffale».

## How it was read

The three juan were bracketed on the 版心 and tiled at 50 dpi on the first day,
and six readers took a range each to a brief that asked for every worked case
and every passage on laying a board. Five reported. The sixth, sheets 47–64,
left renders and no report, and that range was given to two fresh readers on
the second day. The cases of 31–46 were laid with the bench written for 寶鑑摘要,
`board.mjs`, and the passages that bear on a value were read again on the
plate, the 局 table of 卷四 at 450 dpi. 《遁甲演義》卷二 was opened on the 四庫
plate for the cases 卷二 shares with it.

## What it found

**卷二 is the 符應經's 卷中**, in the recension of 遁甲集成 第一冊, which the
大全's own 卷二十六 replaces with a list. So the compilation carries that juan
twice, in both recensions.

**Three cases lay only with the centre's stem carried off with 坤's**: 飛干格,
刑格 and 悖格. The 演義 works the first two on the same boards. That makes
it the second work holding `centreStem: host`, beside the 大全's hour tables.

**卷之二下 is verse and lists.** It states the ordinary 起例, names 折補
in the 閏訣 and gives no rule for it, and lists the engine's thirteen 門迫 cells
as 宮迫, with no converse and a verse denying one. That is the default's
cells under the other line's name, and it is recorded.

**卷三–四 work no case that names a 局.** 卷三 carries 淮陰侯's song, the
探索錄's 符經 again, and 卷四 a table of the 局 printed with slips that its own
prose contradicts.
