## What was read

## What it found

The work flies the 時家 board whole: the earth plate carried to the hour stem
in both 遁, the gates carried over the eight outer palaces, the 直使 counted
through the nine and halting before 五 — which the tables fit on 1027 of 1080
hours, against 554 for a count that skips 五 — a 旬首 in 五 taking the
lodging's gate, and the spirits round the eight 卦 under `fixed`'s names. Its
printed boards lodge the centre by the solstice (`dunni`); its 論寄宮 argues
the four corners by the four 立 (`siyu`), and its 例言 says it printed the one
and left the other to analogy.

Two things the prose does not say came off the drawings: the yin heaven plate
is a translation, which is not what `xingfei` lays under 陰, and the 直使 waits
rather than skipping. The first is a lead against the 折衷's yin, which that
work never works.
