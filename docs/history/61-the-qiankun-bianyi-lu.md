# Phase 61 — 乾坤變異錄

The next work in the 大全, read on the same day as phases 59 and 60 and for
the same question. This is 2026-10-17.

## How it was read

The juan were bracketed in one run by a new script, `blanks.py`, which
lists the sheets with a blank half: a juan closes on a right page and the
next opens on a left one. Each bracket was confirmed on the 版心. The six juan
were seen on contact sheets and edge strips, and none was read on the plate.

## What it found

**Omens of the sky and the ground**: the sun's 暈 and eclipses, the moon
against the lodges, 雲氣 in the sky and over a city, the eight winds, rain,
thunder. Nothing is laid on a 局.

**The work is six juan, not seven.** 卷三十九 is 禽遁秘訣陰符經, so the 目錄's
boundary between the two works is a juan late. The brackets of 禽遁 and the
opening of 奇門秘訣 at sheet 983 came out of the same run.
