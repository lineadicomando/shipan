# Phase 31 — the third volume

Phase 30 left a list of three unsurveyed things and a sentence saying which of
them the shelf most wanted: «The witness to the procedure is still missing, and
it is the single thing this phase most wants.» This phase opened the first item
on the list, 《遁甲集成》第三冊, and found it — along with a second finding
nobody was looking for and a method fact that cost a wrong assumption. Then it
finished the other two items, which took an hour between them and turned up a
work the shelf had not recorded.

So the dunjia survey is closed and `plate: fei` is what is left of it. It also
began by correcting the previous phase's own arithmetic.

## The count that had been left behind

Phase 30 shipped a twelfth 十干克應 pairing and updated `docs/sources.tsv` to
say twelve. `docs/sources.md` still opened its section with «Eleven are
implemented. The other seventy are absent», still marked 庚 over 壬 **not
shipped** in the cross-check table, and still said «Ten of the eleven are
pinned to a line of the song». Three statements about a table, in the file that
argues the table, disagreeing with the file that weighs it and with the code.

The rule that catches this — «Do not write counts by hand» — is enforced by
`apps/web/test/docs.test.ts` for the counts that page carries, and these were
not among them. They were prose. The lesson is not that another assertion is
needed but that a shipped value has four homes, and the register's own opening
sentence is the one easiest to leave behind because it is the sentence read
first and edited last.

## What the file turned out to be

`docs/scans.md`'s rule is that what a file *is* gets settled before anything is
planned around it. Three of the four commands it names were run, and they
disagreed with what the shelf recorded in one way and with the plan in another.

第三冊 is **two book-pages to the sheet**. The other two 遁甲集成 volumes read
in phase 30 were surveyed as though one book-page were one sheet, and on this
one that would have halved the coverage of every contact sheet. The closing
leaf settles it arithmetically: sheet 2 carries the series page 1019 and sheet
306 carries 1626, and 306 sheets at two pages apiece is exactly that span.

There is no colophon. The volume ends mid-section on 出戰 and 伏兵, which means
the extent cannot be had from the last leaf here as it was for 御定奇門寶鑑 and
the third 遁甲演義.

**And the 版心 carries no printed running title.** Phase 30 named two works
inside 第六冊 by their running titles and wrote that method into the roadmap.
It cannot be run on this volume: the reproduction shows only modern hand
numbering at the fold. What a section is has to be read off the heading of the
first table on the leaf, which is a weaker instrument — it names a table and
not a work, and it is why one of the two findings below arrives with its
attribution open.

## 《遁甲符應經》, and the centre's lodging

The volume opens on 直符臨X門應 tables and turns, at its p. 1136, into a work
that names itself: 遁甲總序, then 造式法, 九星所直, 八門, and a 天象分野圖
closing at p. 1159.

Its 九星所直 derives the nine palaces from the 洛書 and then says where the
centre goes:

> 以靈龜出洛，戴九履一，左三右七，二四為肩，六八為足，五在中央者，土火之子、
> 金之母，所寄理於西南坤之位也。

Read at 400 dpi with the region cropped and doubled, because a clause that
decides a parameter is exactly the case `docs/scans.md` reserves that ladder
for.

`centreLodging` ships `kun` and refuses `dun`. Until now `kun` stood on one
witness and that witness was 《圖解奇門遁甲大全》, a modern divulgation — a lead
and never a witness, as the register itself says of it. Now a transmitted text
states the same thing, unconditionally, with no second position named anywhere
near it. The default did not move. What moved is what it stands on, and
**confirming counts as much as contradicting** is the register's own rule for
why that is worth a phase.

## The three 家, and the answer arriving as a negative

From p. 1160 the volume prints 上元 · 中元 · 下元 年家奇門定局, then 月家奇門
tables, then 起日奇按冬夏二至二十四氣順逆分布六局定法 with 日家 tables under it.

This is the object 遁甲演義 lays and not a selection table — one board to an 元,
each 元 running sixty years, cast in 陰遁, with the 直符 and the 直使 given per
year. 《奇門闡秘前編》's 年家八節三奇定局, which phase 30 examined and set aside,
was a table of where 乙丙丁 stand at the eight 節; this is a rule for laying a
board, which is the thing that was missing.

And it disagrees. 遁甲演義 卷一: 「三元年遁：上元六十年以陰遁一局加之，中元六十年
以陰遁四局加之，下元六十年以陰遁七局加之」. 第三冊, in the heading of its first
table: 「以一七四為法」, and then 上元一局, 中元七局, 下元四局, a leaf of tables
to each. Both were read at 400 and 450 dpi with the region cropped, because 四
against 七 is the whole of the finding.

The disagreement is not random. **一七四 is the sequence 遁甲演義 gives its own
月家** — 甲己遇四孟 in 一宮, 四仲 in 七宮, 四季 in 四宮. The two works agree on
the 月家's palaces and part on the 年家's: the same three numbers over the same
three 元, in a different order.

So `system: nianjia` was refused for want of a second witness, and it has one,
and the value stays refused. The standard is two witnesses agreeing or one text
that checks itself; two disagreeing is neither, and preferring the older or the
better-printed would be the engine choosing a school in silence. **That is a
finished errand and not a stalled one** — the roadmap's most-wanted item now
has an answer written down, and the answer is no.

What is *not* settled is whose the tables are. They stand under no title leaf,
符應經's 分野 closes on the facing page, and the 康熙二十三年 and 康熙二十七年
datings in them cannot be a Song work's own. It bears on nothing: a Qing table
contradicting a Ming one is a disagreement whichever work it sits in.

## Then the rest of the survey, which was cheap and found two more things

Phase 30's list had two other items, and they were finished in the same
session because a table of contents costs one look.

**The three 故宮 volumes' 新編目錄 name eleven works, and one of them was not on
the shelf's list.** 第427冊 was recorded here as the photographic edition of
《奇門遁甲秘笈大全》 and nothing else; its own contents page says 秘笈大全 runs
二十三卷 to p. 242 and is followed by 《諸葛武侯行兵遁甲金函玉鏡》, 六卷, to the
end of the volume — in a cursive manuscript hand, not block printing.

That work is the trap this shelf already has a rule for. Its name is 遁甲, its
running title is 金函玉鏡圖, and its board is **not** the board this engine
computes: it lays nine stars from the day — 「假如甲子日巳上起太乙」, with 太乙,
攝提, 軒轅, 招搖 and 咸池 legible around its diagrams — where dunjia's nine are
天蓬 through 天英 turned with the 值符, and what stands beside them is an hour
table grading the twelve hours 黃道 and 黑道 by the day gods. The relation
金口訣 has to 六壬, arriving in the dunjia shelf. It is written into the
register beside `plate: fei`, because that is the parameter somebody would
reach for it to answer.

**第二冊's second half is 奇門秘要, and it prints 剋應 in bulk meaning something
else.** At its pp. 810–811 the 剋應 answers a gate over a palace — 驚加乾用法,
驚加坎用法, 驚加艮用法, each with «what you will meet» under it: 「主老少不和，
宅舍生非，陰人為禍」. That is an omen told to a querent, which is the register's
own example of what does not get imported, and it is not 十干克應 under a
variant name. Worth a paragraph only because 剋應 is the search term, and a
search across this shelf lands there.

## What this phase did not do

It did not settle `plate: fei`, which is now the whole of what is left of the
roadmap's first phase. The 年家 and 日家 boards in 第三冊 are given as tables of
finished positions rather than as a flying procedure, and whether that volume
states the flight anywhere was not established — the survey located sections
and four pages were read whole, which is a survey and not a reading. The same
holds for the two negatives above: three leaves of 金函玉鏡 and one of 奇門秘要
say what those works are, not what every leaf of them contains.
