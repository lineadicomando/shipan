# Phase 59 — 先天要論, the rest of it

Phase 56 read the opening of 先天要論, 卷十七 of the 大全, and found 置閏 at the
solstices. Seven juan were left, to about sheet 588, and the question put to
them was the one the file was bought for: a 起例 the engine does not compute.
This is 2026-10-17.

## How it was read

The eight juan were bracketed on the 版心 first, as the shelf requires of
this file. Every sheet from 420 to 588 was seen on contact sheets at 50 to
60 dpi. Plate reading at 220 dpi was kept for the two places a 起例 could
stand: the run of 卷十七 that lays a board, and the verses of 卷二十 that place
the 六儀. 卷二十四 closes on sheet 588, as the line on the shelf had it.

## What it found

**卷十七 carries 《奇門探索錄》卷三's 符經**, from 遁甲天元金口訣 to 斷數總歌, in
the same order and with the same 按. That run was read on the 2012 print on
2026-09-10, and now has a woodblock beside it. The woodblock reads 都天轉甲 in the
按, where the print has 輔甲, and it has the print's 第盤 too. This is the
second chapter of 探索錄卷三 found in the 大全, after 超神接氣直指 in 卷一.

**卷二十 and 卷二十一 are the ordinary 局.** The 值符歌 walk the 六儀 round the
palaces as the earth plate does. The term table names each 局 by its star, and
the sixty-six seen are all `JU_TABLE`'s.

**The other five juan are 斷, 章, 類象 and the 克應 of the gates**, located by
their headings and not read.

## What it moved

The register only: a section in `docs/sources.md`, a paragraph under the
探索錄's 符經 settling 輔 against 轉, clauses on two rows of `sources.tsv`,
and the file's line in `texts/README.md`. No value, no rung, no version.

## What was not done

穀雨 and 霜降 were not looked for past the contact sheets. 二局's 辛, which the
verse sends to 巽 and to the centre in one line, was recorded and not
resolved. Which of the 大全 and the 探索錄 carries the other is still open.
