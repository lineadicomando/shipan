# Phase 51 — the 《統宗》 of 太乙, read by its juan

《太乙數統宗大全》 had been opened on two juan. This is 2026-10-11: the forty
mapped by their own 目錄, nine of them read, and what the reading let the
engine do.

## What the question was, and what answered it

**The shelf's first line asked for a worked 日計 or 時計 board, and there is
none with a date.** Every board in the 統宗 and in the 金鏡 is entered by its
局 and the 干支 of its day or hour. That fixes the phase of a count — 局 1 on
a 甲子 — and says nothing about which 甲子. The first write-up of this took
it for more than it was and called the day's count settled.

**卷二 then unsettled it.** The 甲子 the work dates as 上元第一紀 stands 120
days, or 240, from where the count on the same leaf puts it, and those are
the two 盈差 the work names for the day. The same write-up had read the 盈差
of p. 29 as refused, off the sentence of p. 31 about the gates; p. 29 ends
用之. `ji: riji` stays refused on the discrepancy and not on an unread text.

## What it moved

- **`yearBoundary: dongzhi` is computed.** 卷二十 turns the year at 子 and
  卷二 counts it from one 天正冬至 to the next. No board is dated in the
  weeks the two boundaries part on, so it stands on rung 5.
- **`host.accord` and `guest.accord`.** 陰陽和不和 had been left out as two
  accounts that do not line up. On the 金鏡's 卷二, the 統宗's 卷六 and 盧央
  it is three tests on three subjects; the one that reads a count against the
  seat of its eye is a total function with its count named, and travels. The
  other two do not.
- **Two rows rose to rung 2**, 八門直使 on twenty-five dated years and 大遊
  on one, each a second work dating what the 金鏡 states.
- **文昌 in 太乙's palace.** The 統宗 carries all three names, and files the
  arrangement under 關 in its own list; 關 had been declared as reported.
