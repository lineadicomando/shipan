# Phase 76 — the hours of 寶鏡圖

A session on 2026-10-26, on `main`, after phase 75. The user asked to resume
reading the shelf, and the first item of the roadmap was the errand phase 70's
reading had left: 截路空亡 on the fifty-nine leaves of 寶鏡圖 that had not
been read for it.

## How it was read

The hour lines stand on the half-pages after each day's wheel.
`scripts/leaves.py` rendered the three after each of the sixty at 150 dpi, and
four readers, fifteen days each, transcribed every hour entry to a brief whose
calibration was 戊辰's leaf, doubtful characters at 400 dpi. Transcribing
the whole entry and not only the flag cost little more, and it let one script,
`scripts/hours.mjs`, set the god, 截路空亡, 五不遇, the noble, 喜神 and the
decade's void beside `dayHoursOf` at once. Two 戊 and 癸 leaves were read
again on the plate before anything was written.

## What it found

截路空亡 on a 戊 or 癸 day is 子 and 丑 on every such leaf, twelve of twelve,
where the 歷例 the 協紀 quotes, and the engine with it, give 戌 and 亥 as well.
The 癸未 leaf was the work's rule and not a slip. It is a divergence between
two works and so a reading a parameter could carry; it is recorded with no
parameter and put to the user.

The god of each hour agrees on 526 hours of 590, and the leaves' slips are
named. 丁酉's leaf carries another day's hours whole. 五不遇 on the leaves
follows the hour's branch against the day's stem on most of the hours it
marks, and marks a third of the days: seen, and not weighed.
