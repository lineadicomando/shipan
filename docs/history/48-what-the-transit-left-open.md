# Phase 48 — what the transit left open

Phase 47 closed on a list. This is what became of it, one step at a time, each
on a branch of its own and merged with its series kept.

## The flags, read off the declaration

The command line had a flag for some parameters and none for others, written
by hand as each arrived. They are derived now: a declared parameter has a flag
because it is declared, the help prints them a board at a time, and a flag
given to a board that does not read it is refused by name. `--years` is the
one kept by hand, being a count and no school.

## Earth's eighteen days, on a sibling art's word

`earth: eighteen` had no text. A 六壬 manual states the rule, and the owner
decided that a statement on the five phases made in one of the 三式 is a
witness in another, the arts drawing the phases from one root. The register
says so once, as a rule about phase statements and nothing wider.

## The second pass of attribution

Plates were opened for the defaults nobody had been asked about: who turns the
day at midnight, who casts by true solar time, who counts luck by the 時辰,
whose the second table of the noble is. `yearBoundary: dongzhi` was computed
from a modern 子平 treatise that works it.

**解神 was not a divergence.** The register's ten-in-a-hundred agreement with
`iztro` was two stars under one name: this board's 解神 is the one modern
boards call 年解, and agrees with it on every day of a year. No parameter; the
collision is said where the star is.

## The 授時 frame

`xiudu: shoushi` was to be a table with one width supplied by a sum. It was
laid by the work's own method instead — the 弧矢割圓 conversion, the equatorial
widths, the precession with its 消長 — and reproduces the printed table to
within a 分. A first note had the two frames a few tenths of a degree apart;
measured, they part by several degrees, the lodges of that frame being polar
longitudes.

## The flight

`plate: xingfei` waited on the chapter being read for how it lays. Three
sheets give it: the direction, what flying a 值符 is, and two hours of 陽遁一局
worked to a named pattern each. The engine reproduces both, and the second is
one no turning plate produces.

**What was composed and not read**: no yin hour is worked, so the yin flight is
the stated direction put to a backward count the text makes for another
purpose; and the spirits are left a ring of eight, nothing read having flown
them. Both are in the register in those words.

With it the board the 折衷 lays whole — stars flown, earth plate by the decade,
centre lodged by the eight 節 — is the first combination of three non-default
values that the line under a board names one text for.
