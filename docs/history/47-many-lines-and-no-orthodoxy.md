# Phase 47 — many lines and no orthodoxy

A premise this project never wrote down, and was built on: that the arts it
lays each have a rule, that the texts are imperfect witnesses to it, and that
comparing enough of them brings it back. The register's standard said so in
one sentence — «For anything that cannot be derived, **two** such sources must
agree» — and the ladder said it in another, that the risk it orders is of a
rule being «the one lineage's own».

Forty-six phases of reading did not recover a rule. They found branching, in
every art and at every depth the shelf reaches. This phase drops the premise
and says what stands in its place. It revises phases 17, 33, 34 and 35, which
are left as they were written.

## What the shelf had already said

None of this was new on the day. Each finding had been closed with «nothing
moves», because under the premise it was a problem to be contained.

- **奇門's defaults are one party's doctrine.** Phase 46's own shelf: the 折衷
  names 顧陵岡池本理 for the turning plate and the lodging in 坤 together, and
  every witness the register had for either sits in that line.
- **A plural transmission was a reason to refuse.** The year, month and day
  families stayed refused because their fullest witness finds 「諸說紛紜，全無
  定見」.
- **Agreement kept turning out to be kinship.** The four sources of 十干克應
  were one line in four states; the 統宗 is on the shelf four times.
- **A school had passed for the norm.** Phase 40: 茅山, computed by default
  under 拆補's name.
- **Two works had been filed as two copies.** In 太乙, 《金鏡式經》 and the
  《統宗》 part on three conditions, and the register followed the first on the
  ground that naming a value for the second «would be naming a parameter for a
  recension».
- **The measure was a book, or the sky.** 《協紀辨方書》 was chosen for the
  almanac because it adjudicates, and what it rejects left with its verdict;
  on a 七政四餘 board the default frame is one no practitioner casts by, and
  both that practitioners do cast by are refused.

Phase 35 had already cut the first exception — a school value claims
attribution and transcription, not doctrine. This phase makes the exception
the rule.

## What holds now

It is in `docs/sources.md` § "The standard, stated once" and is not repeated
here. In one line: a transmitted quantity says who carries it and how well it
was transcribed, and «the answer» is only what was measured or derived.

## The decisions, in the order they were taken

1. **A reading attested only by those who reject it is declarable**, if there
   is enough to reconstruct it without contradiction.
2. **Three states**: declared when attributed and its effect can be said cell
   by cell; computed when it can besides be reconstructed and its witness has
   been read; recorded with no parameter while a shape would be a guess.
3. **The ladder is kept.** Redrawing it was decided, then withdrawn the same
   day when it was put beside its cost: every row of the register re-weighed,
   for a change that correcting one gloss delivers. Rung 2 is redefined as a
   check on transcription, the standard's sentence leaves, and a line says why
   a collation stays off. The redraw is weighed again once three single-witness
   school values are computed.
4. **What the 折衷 flies and turns is a third value of `plate`**, not two axes:
   two axes would make a fourth combination no text holds.
5. **New parameters of one art are declared in one movement.**
6. **Which values are attested together is derived**, from witnesses
   registered per value, and said under the board in three states.
7. **`—` is a negative about the shelf that was asked.**
8. **Two works that disagree are two values.**
9. **A manual in modern type is read on the plate**, and one targeted search a
   value precedes «not expected».
10. **`xiudu: juxing` stays the default** when a practitioner's table becomes
    computable: a default is a convention and does not move for attestation.
11. **A source reached through a modern study counts as read**, the study
    named.
12. **A new value is named for its rule.**
13. **涉害's two orders stay recorded**, and so do the lines the 協紀 rejects.

## What this phase changed, and what it left

It changed `docs/sources.md` (the standard, the collation, what a line's
witness is), `docs/notes.md` (rung 2, the risk the ladder orders, the dash),
`docs/parameters.md` (the default as a convention, two works against two
copies, naming), three lines of `CLAUDE.md`, the opening of `ROADMAP.md` § 1,
one pass of the `review` skill, and six strings on the notes pages in each
vernacular. The third term of the version moved, to 0.6.1.

**It left every entry of the register where it was.** The rows of «What is
refused, and why» still give their reasons as they stood, several of them
invoking the bar this phase removed, and a paragraph at the head of that table
says so. Each is reopened when its value is taken up. Rewriting them in one
sweep would have been the quiet rewrite the register forbids, and would have
passed a judgement on each value that nobody had made.

**And it left `ROADMAP.md` § 1's clauses**, which still say of several values
that a second witness is what they wait on. The attribution pass, art by art,
is where each divergence is put in one of the three states.

## The registry, and the pass through every art

Two things followed in the same sitting, each a step of its own.

**Who holds a value became data.** `packages/core/src/witnesses.ts` registers
the texts — a work handed down, a line's modern manual, the engine's own
construction — and each declared value names the ones that hold it. Three
things were decided while it was being written and were not in the plan:

- **A reading reported by those who reject it is its own state**, `reportedBy`
  beside `heldBy`. `centreLodging: dun` is the one value in it.
- **The school column gained a third word, `recorded`.** Once a dash meant «no
  divergence known», a quantity with a known divergence and no parameter could
  not carry one; 解神 was the first row that could not.
- **No year is recalled.** A witness carries one where the register states it.

**Then every art was read for whose each value is**: 紫微斗數, 奇門, 六壬, 太乙,
七政四餘, the almanac, and the layers under all of them. Each gained a section
in `docs/sources.md`, the rows it owed, and a table of states in `ROADMAP.md`
§ 1. No plate was opened and no answer changed.

What the pass found, counted on the day it closed and asserted by no test: of
seventy-one declared values, thirty-eight have a holder named, one is reported,
and thirty-two are not yet attributed — twenty of those computed. The
unattributed defaults are where the old premise had been doing its work
unseen: the cut of the year and of the day, the naming of 奇門's middle pair,
the palaces of a 七政四餘 board. Nobody had been asked whose they were because
nothing had required them to be anybody's.

One test was wrong and passing. The suite holding the school column to the
declaration kept one board for each pair, so 紫微斗數's year boundary passed by
the order of a list.

## Two values computed, and one that was not

The values the shelf already allowed were taken up one at a time.

**`daxian: ming`** went through as planned: the 捷覽's worked example and the
manual's two boards are what the tests assert.

**`huoling: hour`** waited on the plate, as decided. Printed pp. 405–408 give
a diagram and a rule to each of the four groups of years, in plotting chapters
the volume says it wrote for beginners; no school is named for the count. Run
against `iztro` over 734 births, both stars fall in the reference's palace in
every one. A band of scan shadow covers the foot of two of the four pages and
one closing clause was not read through it.

**`centreLodging: dun` was not computed, and the plan was wrong about it.**
The proposal, the attribution pass and `ROADMAP.md` all said the two texts
reporting it agree. They do not: 《御定奇門寶鑑》 sends the yang board's centre
to 坤 and the yin board's to 艮, and the 折衷, quoting 《三才發秘》, sends them
the other way round. Both quotations had been in the register for weeks, in
sections a thousand lines apart, and the 折衷's own section counts its
sentence as «a second statement» of the value. The condition a reported
reading has to meet — enough to reconstruct it without contradiction — fails
on exactly the two reports that were supposed to satisfy it. The mistake was
caught on opening the code, because writing the branch required knowing which
dun goes to which palace, which no summary had needed to say.

## The 折衷, read on the plate

Ten sheets, for five questions the transcription had located. Three came back
as the transcription had them: the eight-row lodging, the two enumerations of
the decade's earth plate, the rosters of the spirits. Two came back changed.

**The decade's earth plate is the author's own.** The survey had put it on
豫章張氏. The plate has 甘國壁 say that 張氏 left a phrase and that he is
reasoning from it where no book gives the example.

**The lodging by dun is two readings, and one of them has a name.** The
commentary quoted at the head of the chapter puts 陽遁寄艮，陰遁寄坤 on a
《元經》. So the reading the engine declares, the imperial print's, is the one
with no holder, and the reading it does not declare is the one with one. The
value was called computable in the plan, withdrawn when the code was opened,
and is now waiting on a decision that the plan had not foreseen needing.

`spirits: dun` gained a holder from the same sitting, and one unattributed
default fewer. The table of the stars' 旺相 and the passage on the 值使 were
not reached.

## The declarations, beginning with 奇門

The plan had the new parameters declared and refused in one stage and computed
in the next. The owner removed the distinction: nobody is using the API of an
alpha, so breaking it costs nothing, and a value is computed as soon as its
text can be reconstructed. The names were settled in the same answer, each for
its rule.

奇門 went first. The centre's lodging became five values — the two by the dun
that had been one, the eight 節, the four corners — and the earth plate laid
by the decade a parameter of its own. `plate: xingfei` is declared and
refused. Threading the host of the centre through the plates replaced a
constant that had been 坤 since the first chart; the canonical board did not
move, and gained one option.

太乙 followed. Three parameters where the 金鏡 and the 統宗 part on a
condition: whose bodies 格 is marked for, which is computed and held to 盧央's
board for 124; what 文昌 in 太乙's palace is called, three names and each
computed; and 格 by a clash of branches, declared and refused on one board and
no rule. A 太乙 transcript had never printed the block of schools, having had
none to name, and prints it now without the layers a board laid on a year
never opened.

紫微斗數 closed the stage with one cell: 庚's 忌 on 天相, as the 占驗派 reads
it, a third value of `sihua` named for the cell as the second is.

## What was not established

The survey of the five arts other than 奇門 was read off the register and
sampled, not re-read line by line. What is new about the 折衷 — an attribution
for `spirits: dun`, a table of the stars' 旺相, two ways of taking the 值使
chosen by the matter — comes from the transcription sold with it and locates
only. No plate was opened in this phase.

## How it landed, and what it left for later

The proposal this was staged from, `docs/proposal-plural-lines.md`, said of
itself that it would go when the change landed, and went: what holds is in the
pages that own it and the record is this file. The branch was merged with its
series kept, one commit a step.

The ladder was weighed again once school values stood on one witness and on
several, and was not redrawn: a default turned out to stand below the value
beside it in two places, which is what ordering transcription alone looks
like, and `docs/notes.md` says so.

Left open, each in `ROADMAP.md`: the flight of `plate: xingfei`; the 授時
table with 參 supplied by its quadrant's sum; a family of `system`; the flags
the command line owes; and thirty declared values of eighty-five with no text
yet named for them, most of them the cuts of the year and the day.
