# Phase 69 — 寶鏡圖

The last work of the 大全, read the same day, 2026-10-22, at the user's
«procedi».

## How it was read

`blanks.py` and the 版心 bracketed five juan. The day leaves were found by a
small script that measures how much of a half-page's ink lies in a central
disc, which a wheel page has nearly all of; fifty-six were found that way and
four more at the juan openings, where a title column shares the page. Each
wheel was cut to a square disc at 400 dpi. Four readers transcribed fifteen
each to a brief that fixed one wheel as a calibration, and every reader agreed
with it. A script laid all sixty days off the engine's step functions under
both dun and four ways of turning the gates, and a second found for each
wheel the day it fits best.

## What it found

The wheels are the 日家 board. Their stars are the engine's yang count on every
day of the cycle, and the work has no yin half. Their gates keep 休門's walk
of three days a palace and turn the other seven with the sun every day, which
neither `fei` nor `zhuan` lays. Twenty wheels in a run print the day before's
rings around the right centre, a fault of the cutting. The hours of one leaf
read were the engine's 黃黑道, with 截路空亡 at two hours where the engine
marks four.

A section in `docs/sources.md`, two rows of `docs/sources.tsv` extended with
no rung moved, the file's row in `texts/README.md`, the shelf's line and the
next steps in `TODO.md`. No code and no version: the two readings were put to
