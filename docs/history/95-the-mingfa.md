# Phase 95 — 《奇門遁甲鳴法》

2026-11-05, on the user's word («ok, procedi»), after phase 94: line 7 of
`docs/open.md` § "The shelf", the 鳴法's flight. The argument is

## What was read, and how

The file is 60 photographs of the Liaoning Library manuscript, two to a page,
extracted at their own resolution with `pdfimages`. The first 30 openings
were surveyed, and the last 20 are talismans and spells. Read on the plate:
the preface's date and heading, the 目錄, 卷上 第一章 (the board's 起例, in
verses glossed in prose), and the worked case with its two diagrams. The web
transcription on qimenpai.com was fetched as a finding aid. The diagrams'
cells are in `texts/qimen/mingfa/cases.tsv`, and `scripts/cases.mjs` holds
them to the stated rule and to the engine's carried heaven plate.

## What it found

- The order 「休死傷杜中開驚生景」, which the `shushu` skill and the shelf had
  from the web, is the modern annotator's. The manuscript has
  「休死傷與杜，五開驚生景」: 五 is a seat, and it carries no gate.
- The heaven plate is the 法竅's, carried whole in both 遁. The gates are
  carried through nine seats, so the 直使 may stand in the centre
  (「直使為五人罕知」). The spirits are nine and flown. Nothing is lodged.
- The two worked hours, 陰遁二局 and 陽遁二局 丙午, fit that rule in all 34
  cells. The 陰 hour is one where the 法竅's 直使 would wait on 乾.
- The 局 is stated as 拆補, with an example not yet reconciled.
