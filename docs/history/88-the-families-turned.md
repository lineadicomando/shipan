# Phase 88 — 年家 and 月家 read on the plate, and found turned

2026-11-05, step 1 of the build commissioned on 2026-11-02: two plate checks
before any code.

## What was read

- **《遁甲演義》卷一 on the 四庫 plate**, `texts/qimen/dunjia-yanyi-skqs.pdf`,
  at 400 dpi: the 年家 in the lower block of PDF p. 3 (810-923) and the 月家 in
  the upper block of p. 4 (810-924). Until now the two had been read only in
  the Wikisource copy. The plate gives 「將甲頭直符之星逐年干轉，直使隨年支逆飛」
  and 「次就其年遁月建係某甲頭直符逐地下月干轉，符上之門為直使，隨月支飛泊」.
  The month's 「三箇月一移」 is on the plate as transcribed.
- **The 統宗's 附月奇門起例**, 故宮 p. 20, upper block, on the file's native
  image (983 × 1530 px at PDF p. 24; the 600 dpi render added nothing). The
  中元's 局 is 四, and the procedure closes 「即以天盤直符加之，與年奇門同」.
- **The 統宗's 又一本遁月奇法**, 故宮 p. 21, to place the flown enumeration
  the 飛盤 refusal row had quoted as the month's.

## What it settles

Both holders turn the heaven plate on the year and on the month. Their 飛 is
the earth plate laid through the nine and the 直使 counted by branch, which
are the 時家's own steps. So the boards are the 時家 procedure on another
pillar, and `plate: fei` has no part in them. The register had called all
three families flown since 2026-08; that holds for the 日家 alone. The
enumeration through the centre belongs to a variant copy's month method that
places the 奇 and lays no stars.
