# Phase 49 — the hours of a day

The board of a day waited on what its one table prints beside the gates and
the stars: twelve hours, each marked 黃道 or 黑道 and flagged, and a bearing
for 喜神. This is how they were laid, 2026-10-07.

## The table was the bench and the almanac was the witness

《日家奇門》 states no rule, so nothing could be laid on it. Every quantity in
its column turned out to be an entry of 《協紀辨方書》卷七 that the almanac
layer had listed in § "義例五 and 義例六" and left out for being an hour and
not a day. A board of a day is the one place twelve hours belong, so they
were read on the 四庫 plate and carried there, under `shensha`.

## What memory would have got wrong

**截路空亡 is four hours on 戊 and 癸, not two.** The 歷例 says 「戊癸日子丑戌亥
時」 and the 考原's rule, an hour whose stem is 壬 or 癸, gives the same four.

**喜神 is counted from 寅.** The entry derives it by 五虎元, the month's rule,
which gives a day one such hour; counted by the hour's own rule 乙 and 庚 would
have two.

**The noble's two branches were assumed concordant and are not, on one stem.**
The two tables of `liuren.guiren` and the 協紀's own seat the same pair under
every stem, and the first draft flagged an hour on that ground. The bench
then parted on every 庚 day: the table seats 庚 with 辛, 「庚辛逢馬虎」. The
flag stands on the three texts held and the other reading is recorded.

## What the reading moved besides

The 協紀's 起例 for 天乙貴人 is `guiren: wei` seat for seat, so that row went
from rung 5 to rung 2. The same leaves name 日出入 for the cut of day from
night, a lead for `zhouye: solar` and no more.

## The bench

Forty-three of the sixty days come back whole from the column's text layer.
The gods agree on all of them; the flags on thirty-three of forty, with seven
slips named in the test, six of them a flag dropped.
