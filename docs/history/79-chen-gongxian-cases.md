# Phase 79 — 陳公獻's cases

A session on 2026-10-30, on `main`, after phase 78. The user asked to resume
reading the shelf, if anything was in progress. Nothing was, so the reading
went to the item phase 78 left with a consequence on every board: since
0.41.0 a default 六壬 board names no text for its whole combination, because
陳公獻 holds `shehai: palace` and had not been read for `guiren` or `zhouye`.

## How it was read

The OCR of `liuren-zhinan.pdf` located the 心印賦 增注, the 指掌賦's
commentary on the noble's places and 卷五's 位圖, and all three were read on
the plate: none states the noble's seat by stem or the day's cut. 卷四's 125
worked cases each print a whole board, so four readers transcribed them to a
brief, a sheet range each, and one script laid every case under both verses
and both halves of the day. Each square was checked against its lesson 1
first. The cases that fit nothing were looked at on the plate.

## What it found

The verse is `chou`'s: on the 45 day-hour cases of a stem where the verses
part, 41 fit `chou` and none `wei`, the other four each off by one general.
Every day hour takes the day seat. Ten of 31 night hours take the day seat
too, nine of them between 小滿 and 秋分, and no rule tried separates them.
The engine's transmissions agree with the book's on 124 of 125 cases once two
misprinted days are read off their own boards, and the two cases where
`shehai: palace` and `depth` part both follow `palace`.
