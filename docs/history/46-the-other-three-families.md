# Phase 46 — the other three families, and the witness that was two

A question from outside the code: some software lays daily, monthly and annual
奇門 boards, and what are they. The answer is `system` — `rijia`, `yuejia`,
`nianjia`, declared in `parameters.ts` and refused — so the question became an
audit of what the shelf holds on those three values and what the register had
not put to them.

## What the register said when the phase opened

`system: nianjia` had been refused for want of a second witness, then refused
because the second witness contradicted (遁甲集成 第三冊's 一七四 against
遁甲演義's 一四七), then refused on a different ground again: a third witness,
《御定奇門寶鑑》卷二, agreed with 遁甲演義 and then printed three competing day
methods with a compiler's note calling the whole layer 後人附會穿鑿. The reason
had changed twice and the value had not moved.

## What the sweep found, in the order it was found

**The searchable half of the shelf had never been swept for the three terms.**
Every text-bearing file in `texts/` was grepped for 年家 · 月家 · 日家 and their
relatives. 中國絕學 returns only OCR noise — 此年家破人亡, 早年家境 — and
協紀辨方書's 年家 is the annual spirits, a false friend. What is left is eight
files, and the register had put none of them to this question.

**The third witness is the second's text.** 《奇門遁甲統宗》卷二 carries the
whole 起例 run — 附年奇門起例, 附月奇門起例, 附日奇門起例, the 又法 and 又一本
forms, the two 遁日奇法 — word for word with what this register had attributed
to 《御定奇門寶鑑》卷二 alone, including the worked 己亥年 example and the 按 the
register credits to an imperial compiler. It stands on this shelf four times:
故宮 第426冊's plate, 故宮 第431冊's, 遁甲集成 第四冊's 奇門寶鑑卷之二, and two
independent transcriptions. `docs/scans.md` already carried the rule — 第四冊's
奇門寶鑑 is 御定's frame with 統宗卷一 as its filling — and this is the same
splice one juan on, arriving from the other side.

The count on the 三元 is unchanged and its members are not: two works against
one, where the register had written three against one.

**《遁甲釋要》 was on the shelf and in no entry.** 徐昂, 1939, opening
遁甲集成 第一冊 — the volume the register knew for the 符應經 at its far end. It
comments the 遁甲演義 recension, so on the 三元 it is a copy; what it does is
what a copy can. It checks the year family's phase against 嘉靖 43 and 萬曆
甲午 and prints the sequence as a phrase, 「分值一四七諸局」. It names the month
family's palaces as 陽遁 局 where the year's are 陰遁 — which gives the polarity
claim, held here as an unwitnessed modern line, its second witness. And it
emends the verse the month family is laid by: 「昂按順逆二字誤倒」.

**The day family has a divergence of its own.** 《奇門探索錄》卷三 p. 23 prints
the same 訣 from an independent line twenty-one years earlier, already carrying
all three readings 徐昂 would emend to. On the one point the arithmetic cannot
settle — twenty three-day groups do not close on eight palaces — the two part:
遁甲釋要 restarts from 坎 at 癸亥, 探索錄 anchors the direction on the solstices
and says the field is missing it. `system: rijia` would have to choose there as
well.

**The 金函玉鏡 row was one inference too long.** It was filed as another art
under the 奇門遁甲 name because it lays 太乙, 攝提, 軒轅, 招搖 and 咸池 from the
day. 《奇門遁甲秘笈大全》 prints those same nine inside a section headed
日家九星歌訣, from 甲子 at 艮, one palace a day — which is the start and the
motion 統宗卷二's day family gives under the names 天蓬 and 天芮. Same board,
two nomenclatures. The observation that it is not the *hour* board stands; the
conclusion that it is not this engine's business does not.

**A file already searched had a chapter nobody read.** 《圖解奇門遁甲大全》 was
made searchable for `plate`, and the register quotes one sentence out of it. The
same extract expounds all four families across five pages, and tabulates which
nine stars each of them uses.

**Two lineages hold one reading and disagree with each other.** 張志春's
《神奇之門》 and the 高級班筆記 both teach the three families operationally, with
dated worked boards; they give the month family different palaces. The notebook
adds a fifth family, 分家, a board of the minute.

## The plate survey, which is the part that could have been skipped

Eight files had never been asked. Nineteen sheets of 《御定奇門真詮》 and twelve
of 《開悟之門》 are negative, uniformly 時家. 遁甲集成 第五冊 is negative on eight.
遁甲集成 第四冊 is positive and is the fourth copy above. 秘笈大全's 故宮 copy
lists its 日家 block in its own 目錄. 故宮 第429冊 turned out to hold four works
where this shelf recorded three, and the one nobody had opened prints a day
board.

**And 遁甲集成 第一冊 is the survey's own lesson.** Twelve sheets sampled from
its p. 20 onward reported it silent; the four families are at pp. 9–11. The
sample was taken over the stretch the question was expected in — the work the
entry named — and the answer was in the work it did not. `docs/scans.md` carries
that beside the case it already had.

## What moved and what did not

`system` and `plate: fei` stay refused, and the wording of why changes for the
third time: two works rather than three, an outlier explained rather than merely
recorded, and a second divergence inside the day family that any implementation
would have to settle. The register gains an entry it had no line for, loses a
witness it thought it had, and two rows in the refusals table are rewritten.

What did not move is the rung. Nothing here is a lineage holding one reading.
