# Phase 56 — 奇門仙機 surveyed, and 先天要論 opened

The third line of the shelf moved on from 遁甲通明鈐 to 奇門仙機, 卷十四–十六
of the 《御定奇門大全》. This is 2026-10-16.

## What was asked, and what answered it

**Whether the 仙機 states a 起例.** The 版心 bracketed it on two contact
sheets, sheets 346 to 416, and nine more contact sheets showed its headings.
It does not state one. Its first leaf, on 346, is about when an omen arrives,
and the rest is omens.

**What it does hold is a table of one stem over another.** Each 「X加Y」 carries
a name in small characters, and 卷十五 runs 六甲加甲 to 六癸加癸 in prose. That
is the shape of 十干克應, whose register counts six sources and did not know
this one. The rule on arrivals puts an arrival to the questions already
standing, so the table is recorded as a lead. It was not read, since a
collation of 81 cells is its own errand.

**先天要論, the next work, opens on a 起例 verse**, and that was read on the
plate. The 八十老人騎竹馬歌 states 置閏 at the two solstices with 甲己 and
子午卯酉 for the 上元, and no pin.

## What it moved

The register and nothing else: a section in `docs/sources.md`, the file's row
in `texts/README.md`, and `TODO.md`, where the stem table is offered as a
second thing to read. No value, no rung, no version.
