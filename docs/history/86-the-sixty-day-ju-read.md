# Phase 86 — the 統宗's sixty-day 局 read on the plate

A session on 2026-11-02, on `main`, after phase 85. The user asked to resume
reading the shelf; of the three readings `docs/open.md` § "The shelf" named on
files held, the first was the 統宗's rule for `dayDun: liushi`, declared and
refused since 2026-10-07 on the word of a transcription.

## How it was read

The simplified transcription, `texts/qimen/qimen-dunjia-tongzong-jianti.txt`,
located the passage: 卷二's 又法日奇門起例. It was then read on four plates:
the 故宮 統宗 at series p. 20 (PDF p. 24) at 600 dpi, its 局 number at 1200;
《遁甲集成》第六冊's 統宗 at p. 2836 (sheet 17), found by contact sheet; the
imperial 《御定奇門寶鑑》 at series pp. 43–44; and 《遁甲集成》第四冊 at p. 1872
(sheet 126). Renders are in `texts/qimen/tongzong-liushi/`.

## What was found

Three 局 of sixty days to each half of the year, each begun on a 甲子:
陽遁 一 · 七 · 四 after 冬至, 陰遁 九 · 二 · 六 after 夏至 in both 統宗
prints and 九 · 三 · 六 in the 寶鑑. 超接 and the leap are 「如時奇」, with the
leap when the 符頭 is more than thirty-odd days early. The 第四冊 copy leaves
the number in a gap. **The board those 局 lay is laid 「法亦同於年奇」**: the
旬首's star and gate as 直符 and 直使 on a plate of stems. That is not the
太乙-star board `system: rijia` lays, which is the juan's first day method.
