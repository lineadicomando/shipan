# Phase 52 — the second 立成, 杜 and the 定算

The 立成 of 太乙 had one witness, the 金鏡's 卷三, and fourteen of its cells
were held to be its own errors. This is 2026-10-15: the 《統宗》卷三十一 立成
read whole against it, and the one word that table gave the engine.

## What the question was, and what answered it

**The shelf asked for a second table to hold the 金鏡's to.** The 《統宗》's
七十二局立成歷, series pp. 298–307, prints both 局 in full and both generals
of every row. Read cell by cell against the engine, every value it prints is
the engine's or is contradicted by another row of the same table.

**Ten of the fourteen errata are printed as the engine computes them**,
陰局 46 among them: the one cell the 金鏡's tables could not settle against
themselves. The other four are wrong in the 《統宗》 too — two with the
金鏡's own numerals, two with a tens digit each copy got wrong its own way —
so the two tables descend from one, and the register says they are not
independent.

## What it moved

- **杜 is the eighth 太乙 condition.** The table writes 杜 where the generals
  of a count ending in five stand, and the engine had kept that standing out
  of `patterns` because the only name for it was a modern scholar's. It now
  has a source's, and the user's word of the same day put it in. It is marked
  once for the party whose count ends in five, with the 參將 as its partner.
- **A 太乙 condition may carry no fortune.** The seven of 卷三 are all 凶;
  the 《統宗》 gives 杜 neither a fortune nor a sentence, so `valence` became
  optional on `TaiyiPattern` and 杜 travels without one. The precedent is 對,
  which travels without a `meaning` for the same reason.

## What was not done

The notes beside every count of the table — 和, 重陽, 重陰 and the rest —
were located and not read. The user asked the same day for them to be read
to the character, as the next step of the shelf's second line.

## Then the notes, the same day

Every note of both tables was transcribed — five readers on two pages each,
the disputed cells settled on the plate. The label beside a count reads it
by its digits: two odd digits 重陽, one of each 和, with no 上, 次 or 下 and
with 王希明's 雜陽 folded into 重陽; two even digits are split between 重陽 and
重陰 by nothing found. None is read against 太乙's palace, which is the
question shelf line 2 asked, so the table answers it by not asking it. Where
the numeral in a cell is wrong, the label is the right count's.

The transcription also corrected the first reading: four cells taken for the
engine's are the print's slips — 陽局 12's 天目, 陽局 27 and 53's 客算,
陰局 12's 客算 — and one reading of a broken 三 at 陰局 13 was a small 一 of
the note beside a 二. A reading that holds a table to a computation finds
the cells that disagree, and passes over the ones it expected.

## And the drawn boards

The 144 boards of 卷三十二–三十五 were read for their margins and the seat
of 定目. They print right the four cells both 立成 get wrong, so they were
computed apart from either table; and 卷四's 淘金訣 rule for the 定算, run
over them, seats 134 定目 and gives 118 定算, the rest being the boards' own
slips. It was built the same day, by the user's word, as `ding` on every
board, the eye and the count; and once the boards were read for their six
generals, the 定算's two as well, from the units as the host's are, where
133 boards draw them and the leaf's own case does not put them.

## The version

0.22.0, when the canonical 太乙 board, whose second input seats both of
the guest's generals in the centre, answered with a condition it did not
have; then 0.23.0, when every 太乙 board answered with a field it did not
have; then 0.24.0, when that field gained its two generals.
