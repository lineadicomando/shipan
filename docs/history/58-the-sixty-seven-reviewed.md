# Phase 58 — the sixty-seven reviewed, and a table of 十干克應 to choose

The 仙機 arrived in phase 57 with a second line for every cell of 十干克應, and
the user asked for the sixty-seven refused cells to be reviewed before the
shelf went on. This is 2026-10-16.

## What the review found

**The written ground no longer held.** The cells were refused for want of a
second line independent of J. The 干論 is one, and covers every cell, under
other names. With Y beside them the lines are three, and most cells carry three
names.

**So the question changed shape.** A whole table is one line's. Which one a
board lays is a choice between practitioners, which makes it a parameter: the
rule `docs/parameters.md` puts as «does it change what the chart shows, and
could two people each say which they follow». Under § "What a value named for
a school must show", J's table has what a computed value needs.

The user chose the parameter from three options: the parameter, keeping the
refusal with its ground rewritten, or shipping the dozen cells the lines name
alike.

## What was built

before, so no board's marks moved. `jinhan` lays J's table, eighty cells: 戊

**A mark can carry no fortune now.** Under `jinhan` a fortune is J's and only
where J states one without a condition: eight cells of the sixty-six. The
type, the transcript, the page and the drawing print a mark without one, and
`docs/agent-prompt.md` tells a model not to supply one.
