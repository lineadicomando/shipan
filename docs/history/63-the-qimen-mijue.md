# Phase 63 — 奇門秘訣

The next work in the 大全, taken up the day after phase 62 and for the same
question: is there a 起例 the engine does not compute. This is 2026-10-19.

## How it was read

`blanks.py` bracketed the juan, and each bracket was confirmed on its
closing leaf. Every sheet from 983 to 1116 was seen on contact sheets at
50 dpi. Read on the plate: a worked case in 卷四十五, the 局 table and
the 置閏 of 卷四十六, and the 入墓 and the eight spirits of 卷四十七.

## What it found

**The work is six juan, not seven.** 寶鑑摘要 opens 卷四十九, a juan earlier
than the 目錄 has it.

**卷四十六–四十八 are a commentary on the 煙波釣叟歌.** Its verse gives the
上元 of every term as the engine does. Its table gives all three 元 as the
engine does in every term but 芒種, which it prints as 夏至's row. It states
置閏 at nine days and at 芒種 and 大雪, which is the engine's pin. One case
in 卷四十五 names 小暑中局 and walks 陰遁八局, which is 小暑's 上元. No
method lays it on any day the case could be.

**Two readings the engine does not compute.** The commentary seats the
eight spirits from the earth-plate 直符 at a 己 to 癸 hour. The compilation's
own hour tables, 卷五–十, lay 38 of 39 such claims where the engine does. It
also quotes 三璋 for 乙 buried in 乾 and calls the engine's 乙 in 坤 wrong.
Each is one commentator's reading.
