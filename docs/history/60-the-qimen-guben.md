# Phase 60 — 奇門古本, and a second copy of the 年家 on 一七四

Phase 59 left the 大全's next work unopened: 奇門古本, 卷二十八–三十二. It
was read for the same question, a 起例 the engine does not compute. This is
2026-10-17, the same day.

## How it was read

The five juan were bracketed on the 版心, sheets 659–759, and every sheet was
seen on contact sheets at 50 dpi. 卷二十九 opens on 論三元甲子, a statement of
the 三元 of years, months and days, and its three sheets were read on the
plate at 220 dpi, with one leaf at 500.

## What it found

**The 年家 on 一 · 七 · 四, with 遁甲集成 第三冊's epoch chain.** The chain
runs from 元泰定元年 to 天啟四年, and the 大全 works 順治五年 on it. The hour
board's 起例, applied to years, gives that year's gate and star. Until now the
reading stood on 第三冊's tables alone, dated 1684. Two characters part
between the copies: the block leaves out 泰 and prints 弘治十八年 for 十七.

**A 日家 of six 局 a year**, each of four terms and sixty days, begun on a 甲
or 己 day and laid with a plate of stems.

**The rest is omens**: the 八門 and 九星 by palace, 九遁, the 詐 and 假, and
three juan of 雜占. The last leaf of 卷三十一 is printed 卷三十終.
