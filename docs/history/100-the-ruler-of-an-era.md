# Phase 100: the ruler of an era

2026-11-06, on the user's word («procedi con il prossimo»), taking line 1 of
`docs/open.md` § "The shelf": the 量天尺. Nothing was built, and nothing
shipped.

## What was read

- 《張果星宗》 卷569 in the 集成, djvu p. 45, at full resolution. The page
  holds two grids. 逐年行限度法 gives the time a limit takes to reach each of
  thirty degrees, palace group by palace group, and is arithmetic on the
  rates of 定行限度法. 欽天監校正 授時過宮度 is twelve palaces by thirty, its
  cells lodge-degrees. Its first column was read, at the limit of the scan,
  and its layout is not yet understood.
- 《星度指南》, typeset, 第二篇 and 第七篇. It states the 量天尺 as a ruler
  of an era, cut anew as the lodges move, with rows that make degrees 同絡.
  It prints its own of 1864, cut at the 中氣, and works the 丙戌 case of 1886
  through 命度, start and limits.

## What it found

- **Under `gong: zhongqi` the ruler is derived, not transcribed.** A row is a
  distance into the palace. The engine's default board for the 丙戌 birth puts
  the 命度 at 軫 4.2°, which is the book's 軫四, and 霜降 thirteen days and
  8.4 hours before the birth, which is its 十三日四時.
- **The 張果星宗's table is wanted only for `gong: ci`**, where the palaces
  are unequal and a row needs a rule.
- **Two counts of the start are on the shelf**: degrees from the 命度, and
  days from the 中氣.
- The chart printed in the typeset edition is a Moira rendering of 2009 and
  witnesses nothing.
