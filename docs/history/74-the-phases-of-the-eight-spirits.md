# Phase 74 — the phases of the eight spirits

Surface work and one reading on 2026-10-25, on `main`, at the user's request
and on their word at each step.

## The surface

The user reviewed the text on the boards in the browser and asked for four
things in turn.

- **Text set at a size it can be read.** The drawings shrank whatever did not
  fit with no floor under it: 奇門's configurations along the foot of a
  palace, 紫微斗數's crowded seats, the notes under 七政四餘 and 太乙. The
  configurations wrap onto as many as three lines, a crowded seat sets its
  stars in two columns under a glossed lead, and a bare drawing leaves out
  the notes and the conditions the page sets in type under it.
- **The key to the names under the board.** It stood in the column beside the
  board, under the lens, and moved whenever the lens's controls grew. A gloss
  of what the lens kept in view was tried in the column and taken out the same
  day for the same reason. The key now stands under the board, in a row the
  column spans, every entry carrying its word beside its hanzi and reading,
  and it recedes with the board when a lens is laid.
- **The cursor over a name** is a link's hand; it was a question mark.
- **The scan's board has its lens.** The dialog in which a scan opens one hour
  lays the board on a bench as the sections do: the lens beside it, the key
  under it, and the reading that used to stand in the column under both. The
  lens is put away when the dialog closes, so the scan's address is left as
  it was found.
- **The spirits in the lens.** Asked why the spirits took part in no relation,
  the answer was that no phase was registered for them, though the shelf held
  a page giving one.

## What was read

Sheet 1086 of 《御定奇門大全》 was already rendered for `spiritSeat`. The
shelf was searched for the same list, and three answers came back, which made
it a parameter to put to the user rather than a quantity to enter. Each was
read on the plate: the 奇門秘訣's list on sheet 1086; 幺學聲 2005 p. 7, which
gives the same eight phases and refuses them any part in 生剋; and the
九神主論章 of the 秘笈大全, 卷六, found in the 省思堂 woodblock at pp.
104–106 by OCR of the pages around the transcription's position, which parts
from the 奇門秘訣 at 值符 and 螣蛇. 《奇門探索錄》 carries the 秘笈大全's words
and was left located, not weighed.

## What was built

`spiritPhase`, three values, all computed: `none` the default, so no board
already laid answers differently in what it relates; `branch`; `huaqi`. Only
the lens reads it. The drawing leaves the spirits uncoloured under all three,
and the 奇門 fascicle moved by the field and nothing else.

## What it leaves

The 十二天將 of 六壬 are kept by kind on the same grounds the spirits were,
and 六壬's own texts are where a phase for them would be read. The 太常 of the
flying line takes, in the 九神主論章, the phase of whatever it meets; nothing
here lays the flying line, so nothing reads it.
