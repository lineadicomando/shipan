# Phase 55 — the eye of the 大遊, counted two ways

The eye of the 大遊 was decided as a parameter on 2026-10-14 and built on
2026-10-16. The user named it the same day.

## What it is

Both works walk the eye from 天道 over the sixteen gods, holding it twice at
乾 and 坤, and both reduce by eighteen last. They part on the count before
that. The 金鏡 reduces the 大遊's own 積年 by its 元法 of seventy-two. The
《統宗》 adds a 盈差 of 216 to the 積年 太乙 is counted on and reduces by 188.
`dayouEye` carries the choice: `jinjing`, the default and what the engine had
always drawn, and `tongzong`. The values are named for the works, as `epoch`
is, and the name beside each is the word its count turns on, 元法 and 盈差.

## What holds it

Neither work dates a seat of the eye. The 《統宗》 prints its count for 1520,
and that count leaves on 188 what the 金鏡's 積年 for 1520 leaves with 216
added. The test holds that residue and the seat it walks to, 呂申. The 金鏡's
count is held by nothing past its transcription. The two rows in
`docs/sources.tsv` are on rungs 4 and 5 for that reason.

## What was corrected

The register said the two counts agree in about one year in eight, measured
over six thousand years. Over the 3384 years in which seventy-two and 188 meet,
which is the whole of how they stand to each other, they agree in 376: one in
nine. The test asserts the 376.

## The surfaces

The CLI and the web section read the parameter off the declaration and offer
it with nothing written for them: `--dayou-eye` and the form's own line. The
MCP tool takes `dayou_eye`, and a test moves the eye under it and holds the
palace of the circuit still. The line naming the schools says which count is
in force.

## The version

0.26.0. The canonical 太乙 boards carry `dayouEye` among their options, which
is an answer they did not give before. No seat on either moved.
