# Phase 62 — 禽遁秘訣陰符經

The next work in the 大全, taken up the day after phases 59–61 and for the
same question: is there a 起例 the engine does not compute. This is
2026-10-18.

## How it was read

The four juan had been bracketed by `blanks.py` during phase 61. This time
each bracket was confirmed on its closing leaf. Every sheet from 894 to 983
was seen on contact sheets at 50 dpi. The one 遁甲 run in the work, sheets
921–926 and 930, was read on the plate at 220 dpi.

## What it found

**The work is 演禽**: the seven luminaries, the twenty-eight lodges and the
禽 of a day or an hour, read for a battle. It ends on omens of the sky and
of animals.

**卷四十 carries a 遁甲 run, and the run states the ordinary 起例.** Its
遁甲歌 opens the year at the eight nodes' 上元 palaces and reads the 元 off
the 符頭. A 五不擊 works 陽遁八局 辛卯 and names five palaces. The engine
lays all five there, on the plate of the 元. The plate laid anew at every
decade puts the 直符 elsewhere.
