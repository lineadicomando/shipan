# Phase 50 — the 符應經 read whole

《遁甲符應經》 had been opened for one clause and then for its 卷上. This is
the rest of it, 2026-10-08: what the reading found, what it moved, and the
two things the earlier entries had wrong about the file.

## What the file was not

**卷上 did not end where the register said.** The entry before this one had
卷中 opening «about p. 409». The 目錄 had been read and its section list was
on the page; the boundary was taken from where the reading stopped and not
from the list. 卷上 closes at p. 425, sixteen pages on, and those pages carry
門迫, 伏吟 and the four spirits.

**And the two copies are not one text from 卷中 on.** The 卷上 collation had
found one passage the 大全 adds, dated two centuries after the work. From
卷中 the 大全 sets a 奇門四十格 list where 第一冊 has its sections and cuts
its juan at different sections, which the 版心 does not show: it reads
遁甲符應經 on every leaf of both. A line of that list is the list's.

## What it moved

Twelve boards the work lays in prose were laid by the engine from the 局 and
the hour the text names, and every seat agreed. That is the first time a
transmitted text has checked the turn, the flight of the 使, the lodging and
the spirits together, and it is one text: the row keeps its rung.

**One cell parted, and became a parameter.** `TOMB` held 丁 in 艮 on the
統宗's list; the 符應經 has it in 乾 with 丙, under a named voice, in both
copies. It was recorded first and declared the same day as `dingTomb`, with
the 統宗's cell the default. The value is named for the palace. Naming it for
a rule — the phase's tomb against the stem's — was considered and dropped:
neither text gives 丁 a reason, and a name would have supplied one.

**The work is named as a holder of the default lodging.** It works
`centreLodging: kun` in a yang board and in a yin one, and reads the 值使
there, which is `centreTravel: stay`.

## What stayed out, and why

**三奇得使.** The work states a pairing and works four boards. Laid by the
engine, one of the four is the board the text describes. A rule whose own
cases contradict it three times in four is a statement and not yet a
procedure, so the absence stands as it did.

**The centre's stem at 坤.** One 格 is marked by the stem lodged there and
not by 坤's own. It is one case, corrupt in one copy, and it sits beside the
question 《奇門探索錄》 raises on the heaven plate. Recorded, no parameter.

The second term of the version moves: a 奇門 board answers with an option it
did not carry.
