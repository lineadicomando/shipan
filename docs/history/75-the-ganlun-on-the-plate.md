# Phase 75 — the 干論 on the plate

A session on 2026-10-25, on `main`, after phase 74. The user asked to resume
the roadmap and, at the first answer, to take its first two items and to
accept the copy proposed.

## The surface, first

Two faults the roadmap carried were mended before the reading. The marks the
code set between a school's label and its value, between a solar term and its
instant, and between two captions of a drawing are catalog keys,
`cli.between.*`: an em dash in English, and in Italian a space, a comma and a
middle dot. The Italian labels were found to be written as the opening of a
sentence the value closes, so a space joins them better than any mark; the
four that were questions or ended on a preposition were rewritten on the
user's word. And the Italian lens strings no longer put a bare preposition in
front of a name whose gloss opens on an article.

## What was read

The two items were the 十干克應 cells two lines name alike beyond the
fourteen, and the six cells of the 仙機's 干論 the register called garbled.
The second was read first, on the woodblock of 《御定奇門大全》, and the prose
under each entry turned out to name its own pair, which made it the check a
heading had wanted. Two cells settled, one confirmed empty, one turned out to
be the rule of 丁's 遁 and not a cell, and two stayed unreadable without
guessing. Two more the register had not listed were found: 乙 over 戊 has no
entry, and three names stand at two cells each.

The first item was then narrowed by the register's own rule. Of about a dozen
candidates, four rest on the third block of 《元靈經》, whose heading the
transcription gives as 丙 and the cells as 丁's, and that block is not cited
until a print is held; the transcription on the shelf was checked and heads it
the same way. Two pairs part by a character or a word. Two cells are named
alike by two independent lines exactly.

## What was decided, and by whom

recommended:

- the default lays 青龍入雲格 at 戊 over 乙 and 青龍華蓋 at 戊 over 癸, with no
  fortune, since every line hands both verdicts to the gate. 青龍華蓋 had been
  «the cell to reach for if a fourth valence is argued»; an optional valence

- `stemPairs: xianji` is computed on the seventy-seven cells the print names,
  the four it does not left without a mark, as `jinhan` leaves 戊 over 戊. Its
  holder is `qimenxianji`, and it carries no fortune of its own.

the list of configurations; it now asks `layableUnder`, which reads a set per
